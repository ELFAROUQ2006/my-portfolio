About Me

A closer look at who I am, how I think, and what I'm working toward.

I'm Umar Faruk Yakubu, known to friends and classmates as El Farouq — a final-year Computer Engineering student at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria. My work sits at the intersection of hardware and intelligence: I like taking a problem that involves sensors, signals, or data, and building something that actually reasons about it.

Over the course of my studies I've moved between fairly different problem spaces: monitoring systems for agriculture, AI-driven security, industrial fault detection using both classical and neural approaches, and more recently, retrieval-augmented language systems. What ties them together is the same habit — break the system down, understand every layer, and build it back up so it actually works, not just on paper.

Outside the lab, I'm building UYF GLOBAL VENTURES — a venture I'm shaping to apply an engineer's discipline (systems thinking, cost analysis, iteration) to industries like real estate and tours & travel, rather than staying confined to a single technical track.
